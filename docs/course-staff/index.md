# For Course Staff

Your starting point is **Supervision** for your assigned classrooms. A classroom administrator assigns staff in the classroom editor; editing permission or student membership alone does not provide supervision access.

## Before teaching

1. Open the correct classroom and published version.
2. [Preview the content and workspace](./supervision.md) without creating real learner activity.
3. Check student access with the course administrator.
4. For shared teaching, review [group-seminar operation](./group-seminars.md).
5. Prepare a [safe projection surface](./projection.md) rather than projecting individual records.

## Lead a group through a learning sequence

Use the [complete seminar run guide](./group-seminars.md): check preceding activities and pinned versions, switch from **Records** to **Live**, open the matching run, have students join, release/project findings, share the agreed clinical summary, and explicitly conclude before students continue.

The run guide also covers [attendance correction](./group-seminars.md#6-check-attendance-and-correct-a-missed-join) and [blocked progression](./group-seminars.md#if-the-group-cannot-continue). Waiting on a seminar screen is not the same as joining.

To present test results, you additionally need independent `questionnaires` viewing permission. Use the [test-presentation guide](./test-presentation.md). For cross-course analysis/downloads, request the separate [Stats permission](../course-administrators/results-support.md#stats-reports-and-downloads).

If a classroom is missing, ask the administrator to check your account and staff assignment. Do not borrow a student PIN or a colleague's administrator account.
