# Preview and supervise classroom cases

**Access:** active standard staff account assigned to the classroom, or application administrator. Open **Supervision**, then the classroom.

## Prepare with previews

Content previews use assigned content or published sequence snapshots. A sandbox creates an owned preview attempt excluded from learner statistics. Use this to prepare the session instead of working in a student's case.

## Inspect a student attempt

Use the learner ID (not their secret PIN) or attempt number. Only attempts attributable to the selected classroom are returned.

Inspection is read-only: opening results, enlarging images, and reading reports does not save reports, reveal the student's solutions, finish a case, or update timers. Historical attempts with ambiguous classroom origin are excluded rather than guessed.

**Do not project this individual view.** It is a staff workspace, and images may contain identifiers that need human review.

## Filter the overview

Use time, case, and where applicable sequence revision/activity filters. The minute window selects attempts **started** within that interval, including their current progress. It does not mean "completed during the interval."

Test presentation uses submission time instead; do not expect the two filters to produce identical populations.

## Read the metrics

- Unique learners and attempts are different counts.
- Clinical completion uses selected clinical attempts as its denominator.
- Sequence progress is separated by published revision.
- Known-roster metrics require evidenced membership/enrollment; public-room totals can be unknown.
- Laboratory group orders appear as groups in statistics rather than inflating counts with every component test.
- Item prevalence counts attempts containing an item at least once. Repeated orders are separate from prevalence, and percentages can total more than 100%.
- Idle-adjusted active time differs from active-open time and wall-clock duration.

Creation classrooms report drafts/creators, not ordinary clinical-case completion.

## Access changes

Permission is rechecked during requests and polling. If access is revoked, sensitive views clear. Do not export a report to work around revoked access; ask the course administrator to resolve the assignment.
