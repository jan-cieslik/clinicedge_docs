# Surveys, tests, and learning classes

Start the next available activity in your course sequence. Read its instructions before entering answers.

## Surveys and tests

Questions appear in ordered groups. **Back** and **Next** preserve your answers. Changed answers are saved to a server draft; watch the save status before leaving the page.

Only successfully saved answers can be recovered after reload. If saving fails, keep the page open and retry. On a version conflict, reload to resume the stored draft; unsaved local changes will be lost.

Some follow-up questions appear only after a particular earlier choice. Hidden questions do not require answers or contribute to final scoring.

## Final submission

The application checks required visible answers across all groups and asks you to confirm submission. Once submitted, answers are locked. There is one final submission for that course occurrence, not unlimited attempts.

- **Survey:** returns to the overview with confirmation, without a personal answer summary.
- **Test:** shows a read-only report with your answers, correct answers, correctness and score. You can reopen it later while access permits.

Submission completes the activity even with a low score. Repeating the same test at another course position is a different occurrence, not an editable retake.

## Interactive learning classes

Modules can contain text, images, audio/video, flashcards, accordions, and practice questions. Save and resume your responses as offered by the player.

Objective questions are scored; short reflections are saved for educators but not scored. Matching choices may be shuffled. Final submission locks responses and unlocks the next activity without a minimum pass score.

## Access ended during work

An ended schedule or expired account can prevent saving/submitting. The read-only archive is not an editable draft. Contact your course team rather than assuming a successful-looking page means the final submission reached the server.
