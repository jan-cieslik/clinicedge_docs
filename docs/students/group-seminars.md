# Join a group seminar

Open the available seminar occurrence in your sequence. Complete preceding activities first and use the account assigned to your course.

## Wait, then join

Before the supervisor opens the run, you see a waiting screen. When it becomes available, choose **Start case** to join.

Despite that button label, a group seminar does not create your own individual patient. Your participation is tied to the shared run and your sequence.

## Follow the discussion

The vignette is available first. The supervisor releases more text, images, findings, and results as teaching develops. Everyone sees the same stored shared results.

You can browse released items independently. Your selected item does not control the projector, and the projector's selection does not force your browser to switch.

If staff hide an item, it is no longer available to browse. The vignette remains available.

## Timer and completion

The shared timer is a soft teaching aid. Reaching zero does not automatically end the seminar or submit another course activity.

The supervisor concludes the run. This completes joined participants' sequence activity and unlocks the next one. You can revisit retained released material subject to your course/account access.

If you attended but did not join correctly, ask the supervisor. Staff have a checked attendance-correction workflow; do not sign in as another learner or share their PIN.

## Privacy

The projected display is intentionally narrower than the staff workspace. Do not photograph credential screens or send private classroom/display URLs to others. Use only fictional/de-identified clinical information in the discussion.
