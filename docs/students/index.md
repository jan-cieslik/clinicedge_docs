# For Students

ClinicEdge lets you practice clinical reasoning with simulated patients and course activities. Your teacher decides which content and access method are available.

## Start here

1. [Sign in using your course instructions](./access.md).
2. Find your classroom on the [dashboard](./dashboard.md).
3. Open a [clinical case](./clinical-case.md) or the next [course activity](./course-sequence.md).
4. Complete the handover or final submission so progress is recorded.

Your course may also include [surveys/tests and learning classes](./assessments.md), [group seminars](./group-seminars.md), or an assigned [case-creation task](./case-creation.md).

## Keep your access private

Do not share passwords, PINs, authenticator/recovery codes, or private signup links. This is a teaching simulator: do not enter real patient details into conversations, written reports, or support messages.

For study choices, account issues, or a bug, see [privacy and getting help](./privacy-help.md).
