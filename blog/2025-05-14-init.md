ClinicEdge's documentation hub began as a place to explain clinical simulation and help educators prepare cases. It now follows the work people actually do: running an installation, organizing a course, teaching, and learning.

## Start with your responsibilities

- [IT administrators](/docs/it-administrators) can find deployment, integration, security, and recovery guidance.
- [Course administrators](/docs/course-administrators) can prepare and review cases, publish sequences, and organize access.
- [Course staff](/docs/course-staff) can supervise classrooms and run shared seminars.
- [Students](/docs/students) can sign in, complete activities, and understand saved work.

## Less database editing, more teaching

The current guides prioritize the moderation and administration panel. Structured forms, validation, previews, and publication handle normal authoring; technical instructions remain where an operator genuinely needs them.

If something is unclear, suggest a correction in the [documentation repository](https://github.com/jan-cieslik/clinicedge_docs). Keep private institutional information out of public issues.
