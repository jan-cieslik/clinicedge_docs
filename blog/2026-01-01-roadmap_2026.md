ClinicEdge's current teaching workflow goes beyond individual patient cases. Educators can prepare content in the moderation panel, publish ordered courses, and combine independent work with shared seminars.

## Author and publish through the panel

[Case authoring](/docs/course-administrators/cases) uses structured forms, validation, previews, and review revisions. Findings, laboratory groups and vitals have supported editing surfaces. Routine course preparation no longer starts with editing database tables or application source files.

## Build a connected learning path

[Course sequences](/docs/course-administrators/sequences) combine cases, surveys/tests, [native learning classes](/docs/course-administrators/learning-classes), and seminars. Publication snapshots content; started learners retain their assigned version.

## Teach together

[Group seminars](/docs/course-staff/group-seminars) let supervisors release shared findings, record the discussion, and conclude a run. Students browse released material independently of the [projected display](/docs/course-staff/projection).

## Simplify access responsibly

The current platform uses native accounts and PocketBase, with local email/password access, ID/PIN cohorts, private registration links, scheduled longitudinal access, and LTI integration.

Research workflows have separate document, consent, withdrawal and offline-key responsibilities. Read the [administrator guides](/docs/it-administrators) and [course access guide](/docs/course-administrators/access-registration) rather than treating every new capability as enabled by default.
