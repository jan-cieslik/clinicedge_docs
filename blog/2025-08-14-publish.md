ClinicEdge is available as an open-source clinical simulation platform under the MIT License. Institutions can inspect the implementation and adapt it to their teaching requirements.

## Explore and contribute

The current application is at [jan-cieslik/clinicedge](https://github.com/jan-cieslik/clinicedge); this website is maintained in the [documentation repository](https://github.com/jan-cieslik/clinicedge_docs).

The MIT License permits use, modification, and redistribution subject to its terms. Review separate licenses and permissions for medical references, clinical assets, and third-party services; an application license does not automatically license every teaching image or dataset.

## Bring ClinicEdge to an institution

Start with [installation](/docs/it-administrators/installation), then delegate [course administration](/docs/course-administrators) and [teaching](/docs/course-staff) through the supported panel.

Current courses can combine simulated cases, questionnaires, interactive learning classes, and group seminars. The installation, available content, permissions, and institutional approvals determine which workflows are ready for use.

For research, distinguish educational progress from evidence-backed participation. Review [studies and consent](/docs/course-administrators/studies) before collecting data; open source is not a substitute for ethical review or privacy approval.
